Every session

Wherever you started it

Pelican reads Claude Code’s own transcripts. The sessions you began in the terminal, in Claude Desktop or in your editor are all here, live while they run, with their whole history back past every compaction.

  • Live. A session another app is working on shows up as it works, and you can see which app. Once it is idle, continue it here.
  • Whole history. Scroll up to the first prompt, through each compaction, with what Claude kept.
  • Three ways to read. Just the replies, the replies with steps folded, or every step open.

All of Claude Code

What you do at your desk, from your phone

Pelican drives Claude Code through the official Agent SDK, so your login, settings, CLAUDE.md, skills and MCP servers all apply. On top of that, everything you would reach for in the terminal.

Approve with the diff

Every edit and command waits for you, shown the way you would review it. Or let Claude work: plan, accept edits, auto, bypass.

Steer or queue

Tell Claude something while it works and it reads it at its next step, or line up what comes after this turn.

! commands

Run a command in the session’s folder. The output lands in the conversation, and Claude reads it with your next message.

Changes and files

Each changed file with its diff, and the whole folder to browse, read and mention.

Visuals and pages

Charts and diagrams Claude draws, and the pages it writes, rendered in place, in a sandbox.

Rewind and branch

Put files back the way they were before any prompt, or change an earlier prompt and send it again on a branch.

Every model and mode

Opus to Haiku, effort up to max, fast mode and ultracode; / commands, @ files and subagents, photos and files.

Notifications

A push when Claude finishes or needs you, for the work you send from Pelican. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app.

Yours

Nothing in between

Pelican is one small Node process next to Claude Code. No service sits between your phone and your computer, there is no account to make, and your sessions stay where Claude Code keeps them. Claude Code itself talks to Anthropic, or your provider, as it always does.

Questions

Before you install it

Does it work with my Claude plan?
Pelican runs Anthropic’s own Claude Code, unmodified, on your computer, signed in the way you signed in to claude; it never sees your credentials, and it is for your own use. Signed in with a plan, what you send from Pelican draws from that plan’s limits. The details
What if my computer sleeps?
A computer that is asleep runs nothing, and Pelican cannot wake it. While Claude works, Pelican keeps it awake; keep it awake yourself to reach Pelican at any time.
Does it cost anything, or phone home?
No. Pelican is free and MIT licensed, with no account and no telemetry. It talks to your browser and, for notifications, to the browser’s push service, with the content encrypted.
Why is the install about 150 MB?
The Agent SDK, which Pelican runs Claude Code through, brings its own copy of Claude Code. The live demo needs no install at all.
